**Body:**

>

- **Yours to measure.** Every tap, scan and button press is counted.

## 4. How it works (3 steps)

**Heading:** Up and running the same day.

1. **Get your card or stand.** A Smart Card for your pocket, a Smart Stand for your
   counter or table. Both arrive ready to use.
2. **Build your profile.** Add your logo, contact details, WhatsApp, review link, socials

3. **Start tapping.** Customers tap or scan, act instantly, and you watch it all in your

## 10. FAQ

**Do my customers need an app?**
> No. Tapping opens their normal phone browser. If their phone doesn't do NFC, the QR code
> works on any camera.

**Does it work on iPhone?**
> Yes. Modern iPhones read NFC without an app, and every phone can scan the QR.

**Can I change my links later?**
> Any time, as often as you like. Your card doesn't change — only what it opens.

**What happens after the first year?**
> Each active identity renews at KES 1,000 per year. One payment covers all your cards and
> stands together.

**What if I lose a card?**
> Disable it from your dashboard and it stops working immediately. Your profile is safe.

**How do I pay?**
> M-Pesa. You'll get an STK prompt on your phone and enter your PIN.

**Is my data safe?**
> Yes. We follow Kenya's Data Protection Act, and you can export or delete your data.
> See our Privacy Policy.

**Can I get cards for my whole team?**
> Yes — each person or location gets its own identity, all managed from one account.
